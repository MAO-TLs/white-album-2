# WHITE ALBUM 2 — Version 2.0.0

Project Lead: MAO  
Translator: GPT-6 Astra  
Special Thanks: gambs

Version 2.0.0 brings a substantial revision of the existing English translation by GPT-6 Astra. The work focuses on how scenes read and how closely they follow the Japanese: character voice, emotional logic, recurring dialogue, and the timing of what the reader learns.

## What improved

- **More natural dialogue.** Revisions were compared with the previous English in context, with special attention to rhythm, hesitation, understatement, and each character's voice. More explicit wording was kept only where the source or scene benefited from it.
- **More faithful meaning and perspective.** Corrections address implied subjects, who performs an action, unfinished sentences, and the distinction between a character's inference and an established fact.
- **Stronger continuity.** Repeated lines and remembered conversations were checked across scenes and the digital novels. Callbacks now retain their recognizable wording where the Japanese repeats it; paraphrases remain paraphrases.
- **Better preservation of subtext.** The revision removes explanations and details that the Japanese does not supply, while retaining ambiguity, deliberate repetition, and the characters' imperfect ways of expressing themselves.
- **Updated supplementary text.** Both digital-novel PDFs have been regenerated. The release also includes revised Special Contents, audio-drama subtitle text, and native in-game captions.

## Scope

There are 3,200 changed manuscript rows compared with v1.3.6: 3,197 Astra revisions and three previously approved corrections. The final before/after comparison covers all 3,197 Astra revisions. This is an extensive editorial revision of the existing translation, not a claim that every line needed replacement or that every remaining interpretation is beyond dispute.

The package retains the existing Windows and Wine/CrossOver installation system. Main-game and Special Contents script archives were rebuilt with encoding, binding, and layout checks. Both PDFs were regenerated from the same release snapshot. Revised audio-drama subtitles preserve the existing recordings; audio and video streams were copied without re-encoding and checked for identity.

The movie patcher and its previously released movie translations are carried forward unchanged, including their original embedded credits. Experimental movie-layout work is not part of this script release. The release's translator credit is GPT-6 Astra.

The patch has been tested and confirmed working by users on Windows and Mac via Wine/CrossOver. This release updates the scripts within that established patch system.

The audio-drama revisions use the existing transcriptions and scene context. No fresh listening pass is claimed; recording-dependent transcript uncertainties remain.
